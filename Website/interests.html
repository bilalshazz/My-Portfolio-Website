- Technical Interests

Here are some of my technical interests. These are the areas that I am most interested in and would like to
improve my current skills, develop new ones, continue learning and gain more experience in,
with the objective of building my career around them.

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is the area of my degree and Computer Science in general that I'm most curious about. The complexity of AI and the speed at which it is advancing are what fascinate me the most. I'm very interested in understanding how machines can learn from data and make decisions, as well as how quickly AI is changing the field and the world we live in.

One of my objectives is to learn and understand machine learning properly, from how models are trained to how their results are tested. I'm also interested in the ethical side of machine learning, such as fairness and how much people should actually trust what AI models tell them.

Software Engineering

I really enjoy building projects, especially the problem solving side of building software. I'm interested in how larger projects are planned from scratch, built and tested, and what makes code reliable and easy for developers to maintain.

Learning object oriented programming and debugging at university has taught me that writing good, clean and organised code is part of the job. Designing it well from the start helps to save time later and makes it easier to make any changes, and that is a habit I'm trying to build.

Web Development

I enjoy making websites like this portfolio as it is a way for me to turn ideas into something visually appealing, whilst providing users with information. I enjoy building user interfaces with HTML, CSS and a little bit of JavaScript, and seeing my changes appear straight away in the browser.

Another thing I am interested in when it comes to making websites is the accessibility and usability part of it. I've learnt that websites should be welcoming and work well for everyone.

Data Science

When it comes to data science, I am interested in how data can be used to understand patterns, answer questions and make important decisions to solve problems. I find it very interesting how large amounts of data can be analysed to uncover insights that might not be visible or obvious at first.

I'd like to learn and develop my understanding of how data is collected, cleaned and analysed to make decisions. I also like how tools like Python can be used to work with data, and how Matplotlib can be used with data visualisation to make complex information easier to understand and communicate.

Discrete Mathematics

This is another area of Computer Science that I enjoy. I find it interesting how the mathematical concepts behind computers and algorithms work. It was also interesting to learn how mathematical concepts can be used to represent problems and find solutions, and how much of what happens in computing is based on mathematical ideas.

I'd like to develop my understanding of how mathematical concepts are applied in computing, especially when designing algorithms and solving complex problems. I'm also interested in how some mathematical topics like logic and graph theory are used in artificial intelligence and software development.
